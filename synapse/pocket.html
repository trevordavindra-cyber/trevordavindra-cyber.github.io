The pocket core is a small Synapse brain that downloads itself straight into this browser, once, and then runs on your phone — offline, no accounts, no keys, nothing to install.

Step 1 — pick a brain

The 0.5B loads fast (~500 MB). The 1.5B and 3B are noticeably smarter but bigger and slower. Your first choice downloads once and stays cached for next time.

Step 2 — talk to it

how it works: the model weights download from a public mirror into your browser's storage and run on your phone's GPU (webgpu). first load takes a few minutes on mobile data; after that it opens instantly and works offline. it's a small brain — sharp for its size, not 27B sharp. the full uncensored stack still wants a computer.
