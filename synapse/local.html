Pre-load it all
with one file

Grab the installer for your system and run it. It installs the engine, checks your memory, pulls the right brain and the coder, starts search, and writes synapse-local.json so the site can find everything.

synapse-local — installer
mac / linux: bash install.sh
windows: double-click install.bat

the downloads are big (~15-20 GB for the full brain, ~5 GB light) — the installer streams them, nothing to click through

An uncensored model is also an unvetted one — it can be confidently wrong more often. And it only thinks while your machine is awake.

— synapse local core, fine print
🔌

It sleeps when you do

The models answer only while your computer is on with Ollama running. The GitHub-side agent loop keeps working regardless; this stack swaps the demo brain for the real one.

💾

Disk is the real cost

Plan for 20 GB+ free for the full stack, 6 GB for the light one. Everything else is free forever.

🔗

Wiring

Point the site's local mode at http://localhost:11434 (models) and http://localhost:8888 (search). synapse-local.json holds both, written by the installer.
