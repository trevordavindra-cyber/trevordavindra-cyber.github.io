STRAIGHT TALK

What ORBIT won't do

Never spends your money without your yes. Purchases, bookings with deposits, and anything binding get your approval first, every time.

Never fakes an outcome. If a call didn't connect or a site blocked it, you hear that — not a confident story.

Never pretends to be human. It's an AI assistant, openly, on every channel it works.
