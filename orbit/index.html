QUESTIONS

Good questions, straight answers.

What is an action agent?

An AI worker built to own a job and deliver a real outcome, not just answer a prompt. It plans, uses real tools, and comes back with proof — a confirmation number, a booked slot, a live link.

What can ORBIT actually do?

Research, draft, and retrieve; communicate by email, text or phone; manage schedules; coordinate vendors; create documents and websites; complete bookings or purchases when you authorize them. What it can do depends on its tools and permissions.

Can ORBIT act on its own?

Within the boundaries you set. Reversible steps happen on their own; money, contracts and anything binding wait for your explicit yes. That rule doesn't bend for convenience.

What happens when ORBIT can't do something?

You hear it straight: what it tried, where it stopped, and the exact next step. No invented confirmations, no confident stories about things it didn't see.

Is ORBIT out yet?

Not yet. The site and the scripted demo are live; the live assistant is in build. Join the waitlist to hear first.
