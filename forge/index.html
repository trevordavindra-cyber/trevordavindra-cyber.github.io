Getting it into your game

1

Download the PNG

Tap any asset, then download. Roblox needs PNG or JPG, under 8MB, under 8000px on a side. Everything here already fits.

2

Upload to Roblox

In Roblox Studio: Asset Manager → Import, or Development → Decals. Paste the Image ID onto your UI ImageLabel or thumbnail slot.

3

Icons & thumbnails

Game icons upload in Creator Hub → your experience → Basic Settings. Use the 512×512 preset for icons and 1280×720 for thumbnails so nothing gets cropped.

In your browser on this device only. Clearing the gallery or your browser data removes them, so download what you want to keep.
